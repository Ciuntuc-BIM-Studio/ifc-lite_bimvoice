Hip roofs over any outline: `hipRoofFaces` builds the faces of a simple polygon's straight skeleton (hips, valleys and ridges for L, T, U and irregular plans), and `roofFacets` takes an `overhang` that pushes the eaves out past the outline (`offsetOutline`). The ROOF command takes O and an overhang; command-line keywords accept decimals (T0.3, O0.5), and a long prompt no longer widens the drawing tab.
