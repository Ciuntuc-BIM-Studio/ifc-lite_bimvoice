Roof configurator: ROOF on a floor plan now builds a roof system by default (SIMPLE keeps the single solid) — an IfcRoof of covering planes and timber structure, linked to its contour (reshaping the contour regenerates it). Design › Roof system opens the configurator on the selected roof: an architecture zone (name, wall-plate height, covering, and per edge eave / gable, pitch and overhang, or all eaves at once) beside a structure zone (rafters and spacing, purlins, ridge beam, wall plates, hip and valley rafters, build-up above the rafters), with a live plan (ridges, hips, valleys, slope arrows, numbered edges) and 3D preview; Apply regenerates the block in place, Regenerate / Delete structure act on the structure alone. Edit in place isolates the roof in 3D and puts an editable chip on every edge in the plan. Floor plans draw roof systems on their storey with ridges, hips, valleys and downslope arrows with the pitch.
