Door and window opening symbols in elevation follow the window makers' convention by default: the triangle's apex points to the handle and its base lies on the hinges, a tilt (bottom-hung) sash points up, and a fixed pane is crossed by a thin X. The DIN 1356 convention (apex at the hinges) stays available as a project setting (`openingLines`), chosen in the door and window configurator.
