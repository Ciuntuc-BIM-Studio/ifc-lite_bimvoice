Bridges in corridors: a deck (library profile swept on the axis over a station range, with no earthworks under it) on two abutments of a standard type — wall abutment (front wall + back wall) or gravity abutment (battered back + back wall) — each reaching its own distance left and right of the alignment, its height down to a metre under the terrain (or typed), on a continuous strip footing (IfcFooting STRIP_FOOTING), and turned round with Mirror. Corridor components can be mirrored and flipped about their profile's own axes, and the profile editor mirrors / flips a profile's points for good. The corridor configurator gets a Bridges section.
