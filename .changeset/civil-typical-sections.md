Corridor configurator: typical cross-sections as a project library (four starter road sections; pick one, save the corridor's as a new one, write a changed one back), and components whose profile is shown as a thumbnail and edited in place (parameters or dragged points) and saved back to, or as a new entry in, the profile library. Road drawing tabs and the configurator's previews zoom about the cursor, pan by dragging and fit on double click or F (`usePanZoom`). A floor plan with no view depth of its own now sees down to its level's floor (as deep as its cut height); 0 keeps the cut only.
