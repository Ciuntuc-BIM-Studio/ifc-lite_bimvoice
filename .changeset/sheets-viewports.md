Sheets open as tabs: A0–A4 paper in either orientation with a frame and title block (sheet number, title, project, drawn by, date, scale). Drag a floor plan, section or elevation from the Project Navigator onto the paper to place it as a viewport at 1:1–1:1000; the viewport fits its drawing until sized by hand, moves by dragging and draws the view with pen weights by line category plus its drafted geometry and annotations. Sheets export as SVG at true millimetre size, or print / save as PDF at the paper size.
