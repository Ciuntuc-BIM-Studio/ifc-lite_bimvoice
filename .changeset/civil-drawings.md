Road drawings: `profileDrawing` (longitudinal profile with exaggerated grid, ground and design lines, PVIs, vertical curves, grades and a band table of stations, elevations, cut / fill and the horizontal geometry) and `sectionsDrawing` (cross-section views at sample stations with ground, coloured courses, slopes, components, offsets / elevations and cut / fill areas, laid out in columns), as pen-tagged primitives in drawing metres; `CorridorModel.sectionAt`. The viewer adds Road drawings as project documents (Infrastructure › Profile view / Section views, a navigator folder and their own tab with settings) placed on sheets two ways, as the user picks: dragged on as a live viewport (in the sheet's PDF / SVG / DXF on C-ROAD layers), or drawn on the corridor's plan Civil 3D style with CIVILDWG as editable lines, hatches and text.
