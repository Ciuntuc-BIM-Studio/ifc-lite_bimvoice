Change an element's type from the Properties panel and the Model inspector, mapped to the configurators' libraries: a door or window takes another entry of the joinery catalogue (retyped and refitted to its size, its inherited name and mark following, a name given by hand kept), a wall, slab, column or beam another element type (layers and thickness, or section), a roof system — picked by its roof or any plane or member — another roof type (covering and structure). "Edit types…" opens the matching configurator on the current entry.
