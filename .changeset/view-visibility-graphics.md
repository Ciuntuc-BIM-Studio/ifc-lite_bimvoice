Per-view Visibility / Graphics. Every plan, section and elevation has its own graphic preset and per-category overrides — visibility, line colour, cut fill, line weight and a CAD cut hatch from the hatch library — edited from View Properties and saved in the project file. Layer parts (IfcBuildingElementPart) follow their host's category, and hidden doors and windows drop their plan symbols too. Drawing tabs now paint IFC material colours by default, like the Drawing panel.
