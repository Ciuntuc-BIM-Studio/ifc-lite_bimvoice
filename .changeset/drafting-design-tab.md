A Design ribbon tab and a CAD-style drafting view on every plan, section and elevation tab: line, polyline, rectangle, circle and arc; move, copy, rotate, mirror and erase; offset, trim, extend and fillet. A command line takes command names and coordinates (`x,y`, `@dx,dy`, `@distance<angle`, or a distance toward the cursor), with object snaps, ortho, window / crossing selection, layers and undo / redo. Drafted entities carry custom parameters, map exactly to their 3D position on the view's plane, and are saved with the project.
