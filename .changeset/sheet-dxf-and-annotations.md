Sheets export to DXF (R12, paper millimetres): every line, curve and text of the sheet as drawn, viewports clipped to their frames, on layers by role (CUT, SEEN, HIDDEN, HATCH, SYMBOLS, DRAFT-…, ANNOTATION, FRAME, TITLEBLOCK…). Sheets also take drafting and annotations of their own — text, leaders, lines, dimensions… drawn on the paper with the same commands, command line, snaps and undo as a view (annotation sizes in millimetres), printed and exported with the sheet.
