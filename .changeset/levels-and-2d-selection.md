Levels can be added to a loaded model (`addStoreyToStore` writes an IfcBuildingStorey framed like an existing one, aggregated into the building), renamed and, while still empty, deleted, all undoable; the Project Navigator lists them and creates their floor plans. New floor plans and elevations come from the navigator, and sheets can be duplicated. In a drawing tab, a click selects the model element under it (shown in Properties and outlined in the drawing), and drafted entities get a properties panel with their UUID, layer, measured quantities and custom parameters.
