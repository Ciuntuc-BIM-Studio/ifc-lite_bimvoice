Joinery schedules: a new project item (Project Navigator › Schedules) listing doors, windows or both, read live from the loaded models — one card per configured type with its mark, its elevation seen from the interior with the opening symbols, the hardware scheme (hinges, handles) and its dimensions at the schedule's scale, then name, size, opening, sill, count per level, total, U value and fire rating; doors and windows without a configured type are grouped by size. It opens as a tab, exports CSV, SVG and DXF (layers SCHEDULE and SCHEDULE-SYMBOLS), and drags onto sheets like a view, following model edits.
