Plans, sections and elevations export to DXF (model millimetres, 1:1) from their tab: the view as its graphics style it, with door and window symbols, drafted lines and annotations, on layers by role. Views have a drawing scale (View Properties, 1:100 by default) that annotation sizes follow — a text is 2.5 mm on paper at any scale — and that a view placed on a sheet takes. Drawing tabs stay white paper in dark mode, so drafted text and leaders stay visible after they are placed.
