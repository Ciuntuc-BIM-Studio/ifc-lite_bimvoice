# Lapin is charityware

Lapin is free and open source under the [Mozilla Public License 2.0](LICENSE), and it will stay free.

If Lapin helps your work and you want to give something back, **please donate directly to an organisation or a person who really needs it and whom you trust**. Lapin itself does not ask for donations and does not collect money.

This is a request, not a condition. The licence gives you every right it grants whether you donate or not.

## Why directly

The money goes straight to the people who need it, with no one in between. We are not a foundation or an association, and we prefer not to handle money meant for others.

Any cause you know and trust is just as good. That might be a local shelter, a hospital ward, a family you know is going through a hard time, or a school that needs help.

A few things to check before you give:
- Prefer registered organisations whose reports you can read.
- When you help a person directly, give through someone you know, or through an organisation that checks the case.
- Be wary of urgent appeals from strangers.

## Other ways to help Lapin

Lapin is open source, so help with your time and skills counts just as much:

- **Ideas**: tell us which tools you miss and how you work.
- **Testing**: try Lapin on your own projects and report what breaks.
- **Contributions**: code, translations and documentation are all welcome.
